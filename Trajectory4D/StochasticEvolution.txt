# Trajectics Selection Model (Core Assumptions)

## 1. Discrete Finite Search Space
All trajectic attributes are discrete. The total space is finite and bounded.
Intial creation of seed data is randomly generated.

Meaning that the attributes of the initial 2 billion trajectics were generated randomly.
No selection bias whatsoever.

## 2. Stochastic Exploration Required
We must continuously explore the space of possible trajectics.
Selecting only from existing trajectics is insufficient and leads to stagnation.

## 3. Recombination-Based Exploration
New trajectics are generated by stochastic recombination of existing trajectics.

We intentionally allow broad combinatorial exploration to cover:
- we do this by combining the characteristics of successful trajectics 
- without explicitly recreating a successful trajectic
- we need to explore combinations not yet tested
- repetition of combinations after they have proven sub optimal will occur
- we need this because they can become optimal under evolving opponent behavior

## 4. Zero-Sum Dynamics
The system is zero-sum. Average win rates will remain near 50%.
Winning is determined by the playing of points.
A trajectic can have a WinPct of over .5 (50%) by participating in a majoriety of winning points

We are not optimizing absolute win rate—we are evolving a competitive equilibrium.

## 5. We select randomly by win percentage (WinPct), but consistently retest others as well.
WinPct differences are small (~0.50–0.57) and will always be small.
Roger Federer during his illustrious career only won an average of 54% of the points he played.
Every context will have multiple successful choices, as well as some less successful choices
that can lead, under specific circumstances, to special outcomes. 

We avoid:
- strong weighting
- we only prune initial non-wins, only track once a trajectic has participated in at least one win.
- we compress the WinPct of past generations to increase the impact of each new generation

## 6. Repetition Is Expected
Because the space is finite, all trajectic attributes are discrete, trajectics will naturally repeat.
Repetition is desirable because it increases statistical confidence.

## 7. Learning Occurs Across Generations
Selection pressure is applied via:
- accumulation of wins/counts
- and then selection of the parents of the next Gen primarily by > .5 WinPct
- < .5 WinPct parents are selected at a lower but significant rate

We do not explicitly bias sampling of winning trajectics, we only sample siblings.

## 8. Goal
Stochastic Evolution: We are performing stochastic exploration of a discrete combinatorial space
under evolutionary selection pressure.
